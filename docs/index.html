Overview

Keep the music close. Keep the library under control.

LocalSunoDb brings Suno tracks, local audio, metadata and playback tools into one focused local workspace. The browser is the interface; your library stays at the center.

01

Search & filter

Find tracks quickly, sort the library and return to useful Saved Views.

02

Flags, tags & playlists

Mark, group and organize music without losing the connection to the source track.

03

Local playback

Play local audio with waveform, Loop and A/B Loop tools built into the workspace.

04

WAV variants

Compare versions, link local audio to Suno Track IDs and keep the right file visible.

05

Stems

Work with separated parts and keep stem-related material connected to the library.

06

LS Elza

Use the assistant directly inside Library and Imports instead of switching context.

How it works

One local workspace. Three simple moves.

01

Bring in the library

LocalSunoDb works with your Suno metadata and local audio collection.

02

Find what matters

Search, filter, tag, flag and organize tracks around the way you actually work.

03

Work locally

Play, compare, inspect and manage audio without turning the library into another cloud service.
